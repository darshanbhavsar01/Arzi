# Demo script (75 seconds)

Aligned with `docs/context/04_PITCH_DEMO_QA.md`. Run it locally with `make dev` (replay mode); the event deployment has been retired.

1. **Sign in.** Choose *Continue to the demo workspace*, then *Load demo cases* (seconds).
2. **Case A (before filing).** Open it: identifiers are redacted, **₹67,887.50 at risk**, 4 flags,
   each with its policy clause or IRDAI rule. Click the room-rent flag: the fix and its saving.
3. **Case C (rejection).** Open it, *Draft appeal*: the letter cites the 36-month PED rule.
   *Submit for review*, then *Sign* as a named reviewer and export the signed PDF.
4. **Family.** Share the claim, open the link on a phone, switch to Gujarati, press play.
5. **Analytics.** Show the ₹ at risk by insurer and the recovery funnel.
6. **Close.** "Arzi is decision support. A qualified person reviews and signs every appeal."

Fallbacks: the link serves recorded responses, so no network dependency on the model.
